# Social Media / Facebook Posts Generation Rules

When asked to generate texts for Facebook, Instagram, or any social media platform for this project, always adhere to the following rules to prevent shadowbans or account restrictions due to Meta's strict financial policies:

1. **Avoid "Ban" Words**: Do NOT use words like "Ganancias", "Profits", "Ganar dinero", "Rentabilidad", "Inversión", or "Dinero fácil". 
2. **Mask Financial Terms**: Use alternative or softer terms (e.g., "Puntos", "Rendimiento", "Operaciones positivas"). If you must mention a monetary amount, consider avoiding the dollar/euro symbol directly or softening the context.
3. **No Exact Promises**: Do not promise specific returns, and do not use language that sounds like "get rich quick".
4. **Formatting for Facebook**: Do NOT use Markdown asterisks (`**bold**`) for bold text, as Facebook will render the literal asterisks. Use ALL CAPS for emphasis and emojis for structure and bullets. Do not use mathematical unicode bold fonts either, as they can cause rendering issues.
5. **Always Include a Disclaimer**: Always end the post with a clear Risk Disclaimer (Aviso de Riesgo) stating that algorithmic trading carries high risk and past results do not guarantee future performance.
6. **Output Format**: Provide ONLY the generated text ready to be copied and pasted. Do not include introductory or concluding conversational remarks from the assistant.
